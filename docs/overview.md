# What this patch does (plain language)

Akazukin ChaCha for Game Boy (Tomy, 1995) only came out in Japan. You play ChaCha and her friends at the
Urara School of Magic: pick a lead and a partner, draw an exam assignment, explore dungeons, fight, use
items, and graduate within a week. This patch puts the whole game into English: all ~1,300 dialogue
messages, item and enemy names, battle menus, the report card, exam results, the Diary Spell (password)
screen, the title screen, the Super Game Boy border, the credits and the sound effects drawn into the
ending pictures.

Names follow the official English TV dub of the anime (ChaCha, Seravi, Suzu, Urara School of Magic,
Magical Princess, Selene Arrow). Where the dub gave no name, we chose natural English names
(the glossary lists which are which). Jokes and puns were rewritten so they still work in English.

What you'll notice: text uses a narrow, book-like font so each box holds full sentences; menus and
labels are drawn as whole words. The Diary Spell uses letters and symbols instead of kana, and avoids
look-alikes (0/O, 1/I). One bug from the original game is fixed: when Riiya leads with Shiine,
Riiya now answers "I'm not a dog! I'm a WOLF!" instead of a teacher's line playing by mistake.
