# Akazukin Chacha (GB, Tomy 1995) - naming glossary

Reference rule: the ANIME is the reference. Official English wins wherever it exists;
free translation only where none exists, marked "unofficial: reason".

## What official English exists (researched 2026-11-22)

- **Only official English: the Cartoon Network Asia English dub (1998)**, produced by Creative Products
  (Philippines), aired in Southeast Asia, never released on home media and mostly lost.
  Source: https://lostmediawiki.com/Akazukin_Chacha_(partially_found_Asian_English_dub_of_anime_series;_1998)
  The 74 dub episode titles survive (fan-transcribed from an OOcities site, so spelling is medium confidence).
  Known dub forms: "ChaCha", "Seravi", "Suzu" (= Orin, a deliberate rename), "Urara School of Magic",
  "Selene Arrow", "Magical Princess", "Kyuula", "Doris", "Mousekid".
  The dub's spellings of Riiya and Shiine are NOT documented anywhere I could find.
- No licensed stream, no official English subtitles, no English manga, no English home video
  (en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akazukin_Chacha ; TV Tropes Trivia page).
- The English episode titles on en.wikipedia "List of Akazukin Chacha episodes" are NOT official
  (column tagged "better source needed"); they are literal translations, useful only as fan-standard wording.
- Where no dub form is known, the established fan/Wikipedia romanization is used (Riiya, Shiine, Dorothy...).

Abbreviations in the source column:
- DUB = CN Asia 1998 dub episode-title list, LMW URL above
- WP = https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akazukin_Chacha (fan-standard romanization, not official)
- JAWP = https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%B5%A4%E3%81%9A%E3%81%8D%E3%82%93%E3%83%81%E3%83%A3%E3%83%81%E3%83%A3 (Japanese spellings)
- WPEP = https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Akazukin_Chacha_episodes (unofficial literal titles)

DECISION FLAGS for main session (medium confidence, pick before inserting):
1. "ChaCha" (dub) vs "Chacha" (WP/fan). Glossary uses ChaCha per the official-first rule.
2. "Seravi" (dub) vs "Seravy" (WP/fan). Glossary uses Seravi per the official-first rule.
3. "Suzu" (dub rename) vs "Orin". Glossary uses Suzu per the official-first rule; Orin is far better known to fans.
